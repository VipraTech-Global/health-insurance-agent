# Insurer selection — labelled demo sample

The ten insurers below are the sample explicitly permitted by the brief. They are **not claimed to be the top ten**. A comparable, current insurer-level retail-health premium ranking has not been established. The [official IRDAI handbook page](https://irdai.gov.in/handbook-of-indian-insurance) returned HTTP 403 during this run. Search results that combine group/individual health, personal accident or travel are not a substitute for the requested retail-health measure.

In neutral display order: Aditya Birla Health Insurance; Bajaj General Insurance; Care Health Insurance; HDFC ERGO; ICICI Lombard; ManipalCigna; New India Assurance; Niva Bupa; Star Health; Tata AIG.

Official product/download sources are retained per insurer in the discovery register. Discovery counts represent candidate PDF links, including excluded and older documents, **not current plan counts**. Acquisition success never establishes edition applicability. Unavailable pages and unresolved editions remain visible in coverage.

The live official Bajaj downloads source uses “Bajaj General Insurance”; the sample's shorter “Bajaj” name is recorded under that current source label. No sample selection implies recommendation or endorsement.
