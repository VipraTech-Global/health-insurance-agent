# Ten-minute local CoverGuide walkthrough

Run `bash scripts/demo_stack.sh start`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:3021/demo>. The H-only release contains 16 variants across 12 products from the ten-insurer demo sample. Use a local synthetic account for the walkthrough.

State the limits at the outset: this is a cited-document prototype, not the complete retail catalogue. Facts cards are partial, so the four tested profiles have no fully established fits. Star Comprehensive has 3,564 validated printed chart combinations; other prices remain unresolved. Missing answers and unavailable prices are part of the demonstration and must remain visible.

1. **0:00–1:30 — Family and needs.** Enter self age 35, spouse age 33, one dependent child age 8, Pune, ₹10 lakh sum insured, hospital expense cover. Select room-limit and maternity needs. Explain that the screen checks documented limits; underwriting remains the insurer's decision.
2. **1:30–3:00 — Complete fit lists.** Show all three groups in insurer A–Z order. Expand a reason and its source quote. An unresolved result means required evidence or details are missing. Show the full picker and select Star Comprehensive, Family Health Optima and Star Health Assure; selection is allowed from any group.
3. **3:00–7:30 — Five questions from the fixed answer sheet.** Ask: “What room rent limits and room-category conditions apply?”; “What waiting period applies to pre-existing diseases?”; “What co-pay applies, including age and zone conditions?”; “How is cover restored and what conditions apply?”; “What maternity cover, limits and conditions apply?” Watch each independent plan column finish. Expand conditions and open an exact quotation at its physical PDF page. If an answer is not found or temporarily unavailable, show that outcome honestly; do not substitute a prepared answer.
4. **7:30–8:30 — Prices.** Show the indicative price panel. For Star Comprehensive, manually select composition 2A+1C, age band 18-35, sum insured 10,00,000, term 1 year, tax basis Excluding Tax and Zone A. The printed amount is 25,799; open its source on physical page 44. These manual chart choices are separate from the family profile and do not establish its applicable age or zone. A price requires every printed chart axis to match. Missing or invalid charts remain visible. Do not describe a missing source as a confirmed unpublished price. Prices exclude tax; no discount, loading or tax has been calculated, and the insurer's final quotation can differ.
5. **8:30–10:00 — Coverage and limits.** Open source coverage. Distinguish insurer sample, admitted plan editions, PDF candidates, unavailable sources and map fallbacks. Show the same comparison on a narrow screen with horizontal scrolling. Explain that this is a local demo, without rankings or purchase recommendations. AI answers pass six code checks; that is not an independent AI review or review by an insurance expert.

Four synthetic UI profiles for verification: (A) the 35/33/8 family above; (B) self 70 and spouse 67; (C) self 29 and spouse 28, maternity need; (D) self 45, spouse 43, dependent child 12 and parent 72, with “my parent needs OPD cover” as a typed need. Keep source evidence and unresolved restrictions visible for each.
