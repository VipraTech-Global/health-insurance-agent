# Ten-minute guided-conversation walkthrough

Open <http://127.0.0.1:3021/demo> after `bash scripts/demo_stack.sh health`. Use a local synthetic account. The H-only demo covers 16 variants, 12 products and ten insurers; this is a sample, not the complete current retail catalogue. Explain that quotations and executable checks have different coverage, and missing evidence remains “can't tell”. Do not describe code validation as expert verification.

The demo is one chat. On the left is a list of past conversations ("New chat" starts another). On the right is a source panel, which opens only when a numbered source link is clicked. There are no plan lists, fit labels or exclusion reasons on screen. Comparisons appear as tables inside the assistant's replies.

1. **0:00–2:00 — Details through chat.** Use profile A: self 35, spouse 33, dependent child 8, Pune, ₹10 lakh sum insured, ₹60,000 annual budget, hospital-expense indemnity cover on a family floater basis. Volunteer several details in one reply to show that answered questions are skipped. Skip the optional health details. The assistant asks one question at a time. The budget is optional: “I don't know, what can I get?” skips it for good.
2. **2:00–3:30 — What matters.** Say that room limits are a nice-to-have and maternity is a must-have. “What I’ve understood so far” under each reply shows the profile as read. A nice-to-have never removes a plan; only a cited conflict with a must-have does. The chat does not explain exclusions, and the engine has no “why was this plan excluded?” intent yet.
3. **3:30–5:00 — Narrowing to a shortlist.** One difference question at a time; skip one to show it is not asked again. Narrowing stops at five or fewer plans and the reply carries a shortlist table: plan, insurer, variant, type and numbered sources for the requirements each plan was checked against. A plan the documents could not fully confirm is marked “Couldn’t be fully confirmed from the documents”; do not call it a fit. The customer is never asked to pick plans.
4. **5:00–7:30 — Policy questions and sources.** Ask the three rehearsed questions one at a time. Each answer is a table with one row per shortlisted plan and the quoted policy wording; long quotes collapse behind “Show more”. While it runs the send button becomes Stop. Click a numbered source: the source panel opens beside the chat with the highlighted physical PDF page and the conversations sidebar shrinks; × closes it and the chat takes the full width again. Partial answers and not-found results stay visible.
5. **7:30–8:45 — Printed price through chat.** Ask for the Star Comprehensive price and supply each printed axis in chat: composition 2A+1C, age band 18–35, sum insured 10,00,000, one-year term, Excluding Tax and Zone A, spelled as the chart offers. A price table appears only after every axis matches; its sources open the chart page. “Compare the 3 lowest prices” returns a price comparison table. Do not infer zone, tax, discount or loading from the profile. Budget comparison is indicative.
6. **8:45–10:00 — History, mobile and stopping.** Reload the page: the conversation and its tables return, and it is listed in the sidebar by its first message. Open an earlier conversation from the sidebar. On a phone, ☰ opens the conversation list, tables fit the width and a source fills the screen. Finish by sending “Stop”: guided questions end and the chat offers to start a new one.
