The H-only demo covers 16 variants, 12 products and ten insurers; this is a sample, not the complete current retail catalogue. Explain that quotations and executable checks have different coverage, and missing evidence remains “can't tell”. Do not describe code validation as expert verification.

1. **0:00–2:00 — Details through chat.** Start guided chat. Use profile A: self 35, spouse 33, dependent child 8, Pune, ₹10 lakh sum insured, ₹60,000 annual budget, hospital-expense indemnity cover on a family floater basis. Volunteer several details in one reply to demonstrate that answered questions are skipped. Explicitly skip the optional health details, either in the initial reply or with the Skip chat chip. Stage 1 questions use fixed templates with no AI phrasing call.
2. **2:00–3:30 — Requirements.** Say that room limits are a nice-to-have and maternity is a must-have. These are profile A's unchanged needs. Show the read-only “Here's what I understood” text and the live fit, can't-tell and doesn't-fit groups. Open a quoted reason. A nice-to-have does not remove a plan; only a cited conflict with a must-have does.
3. **3:30–5:00 — Narrowing.** Show one supported difference question at a time, or the explicit stop message if no supported question remains. Code chooses the field without insurer ranking; Luna only phrases the Stage 3 question. Skip an optional difference to demonstrate suppression. Show every remaining plan; never select a subset automatically. Restoring an excluded plan makes it available for comparison while preserving its exclusion and quotation.
4. **5:00–7:30 — Policy comparison and citations.** Use three rehearsed policy questions supplied separately below, one at a time. If more than five plans remain, select compatible plans through a chat message. For the current profile A, show the two fits and two uncertain remaining plans side by side; do not call all four fits. With one to five compatible remaining plans, each policy question is answered across them. Each column runs its own H search, packet, answer and six-check validation chain. For each of the three questions, click an exact source quotation and inspect its highlighted physical PDF page. Partial answers, not-found results and temporary failures remain visible. The interrupted guided stage resumes without turning the policy question into a requirement.
5. **7:30–8:45 — Printed price through chat.** Open Prices and submit the visible Star Comprehensive price shortcut as a chat message. Supply each exact printed axis through chat: composition 2A+1C, age band 18–35, sum insured 10,00,000, one-year term, Excluding Tax and Zone A, using the spelling offered by the chart. The customer may volunteer all printed axes in one reply; otherwise answer each one-field question. Confirm any other required printed axis. The existing chart contains 3,564 validated combinations. Display a price only after every axis matches; open its price citation. Do not infer zone, tax, discount or loading from the family profile. Budget comparison is separate and indicative.
6. **8:45–10:00 — Coverage, mobile and stopping.** Open Source coverage and distinguish quoted fields from executable rules for the pinned card versions. Show the mobile guided chat and horizontally scrolling comparison. Finish by sending “Stop”: guided questions end immediately. There are no customer intake, requirements, profile-editing or premium-axis forms.
