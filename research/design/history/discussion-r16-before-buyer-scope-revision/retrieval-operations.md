# Retrieval operations and all-case mapping

[case-requirement-field-map.json](case-requirement-field-map.json) maps all 200 reference cases and 200 opaque evaluation cases to information requirements and proposed fields. It retains each reference case's concrete information and operations. Evaluation questions, facts, values, source selections and answers are absent; the custodian's safe categories and crosswalk are included. A broad requirement mapping means relevant aspects, not proof every field has been exercised by every case.

## Shared selector contract

Inputs are authenticated owner, conversation, exact customer profile revision, advice request, applicable event times and a pinned corpus. Proposed selectors return candidate disposition, mandatory closure, evidence, known calculations and precise unresolved dependencies. They do not return a plausible product name when material filters are unresolved.

1. Resolve the owned conversation and requested customer profile revision. Load only the latest fact and requirement version per logical key at that revision; never retrieve other conversations by default.
2. Resolve people, roles, membership and relevant dates. For existing cover, follow contract revision, accepted options and individual terms. For prospective cover, retain applicable, excluded and uncertain candidates separately; a missing source is not a contractual exclusion.
3. Select product terms by supported issue/renewal/treatment predicates and historical edition. Join all mandatory bundle originals, personal endorsements, applicable legal modifications and open material issues.
4. Load the full mandatory rule graph for the selected intent/configuration. Include definitions, exceptions, table/footnote dependencies and contradiction records. Resolve mandatory closure independently of BM25/semantic ranking.
5. Perform supporting lexical BM25 and BGE-M3 semantic search within the permitted corpus and owner scope. Reranking may improve supporting passage order but cannot remove a mandatory clause.
6. Evaluate typed eligibility/time/expense predicates and calculations. Return unknown/conditional for missing material inputs. Persist exact dependency and citation sets with the answer's scope.

Each selector is implemented later as an independently testable service with a thin DRF view. It must use bounded queries/prefetches and short transactions. The relational operations below describe the proposed joins and filters; they are not installed SQL or application code.

## Representative relational operations

- **Mandatory rule closure:** filter `CorpusMember` by pinned corpus and selected policy version; traverse `PolicyRuleLink` for intent-relevant roots, collecting `PolicyRuleEvidence`, `EvidenceSpan`, `PolicyRuleTableCell` and `PolicyVersionDocument`. A blocked root, missing required evidence or unresolved required target is an explicit blocked capability. The evaluator records the visited policy-rule IDs and exact unresolved links.
- **Historical applicability:** query all contract revisions intersecting the relevant event extent, join accepted members/options and applicable terms predicates, then resolve authoritative precedence. Two materially applicable conflicting versions return conflict; do not order by download timestamp and take the first.
- **Claim threshold ledger:** select exact owned assessment revisions, qualifying treatment events and `UsageEntry` postings inside the top-up's own period/member scope. Sum only the accounting role named by the rule, net of validated reversals. Prior payment is separately queried for residual indemnity. Unknown admissibility or allocation blocks the exact aggregate.
- **Quote comparison:** require matching requested member partitions, cover/deductible/room/options and coverage term; bind each quote to its customer profile revision and validity. Evaluate the closed price breakdown and payment schedule in documented order; an unknown material component prevents a complete price ranking.
- **Provider lookup:** match the exact branch by external ID or supported address, then select the insurer/product snapshot applicable to the requested date and scope. Read its exact network entries and completeness. Similar names never merge, and a failed, partial or unknown-completeness search cannot prove absence.
- **Publication:** validate owned turn lease/revision/erasure generation and all material artifact validity under short locks; persist `Decision`, its claims/citations/calculations, publication message and final event together. Reconnect reads existing `TurnEvent.sequence > cursor` only.

Suggested indexes are listed per entity and reviewed against these operations. PostgreSQL B-tree/GIN/GiST/pgvector indexes are proposals; actual selectivity, plans and five-conversation p95 targets remain unmeasured until approved implementation and a representative corpus. Private query statistics, vector scoring and BM25 corpus statistics must not mix inaccessible customer material.


## Additional original-derived operations in discussion revision 6

- **Claim-document clocks (INS-09):** select the owned claim and its exact contract; retrieve submission, recipient receipt, completeness and payment events separately. Resolve the current necessary-document requirements and accepted receipt/form evidence. Pin the last necessary recipient receipt from that set. Load the external rate at the original-defined date, then evaluate only arithmetic with fully specified units, day-count and rounding. A missing or disputed requirement, recipient authority or rate blocks an exact interest amount. The [additional claim-clock case](../reference/additional-claim-clock-case-r10.json) exercises this operation beyond the fixed 400.
- **Independent coverage accounting (OPS-07):** select a sealed `InventoryRevision`, its source manifest, included `InventoryMembership` rows and immutable support reviews. Require source-scope closure and adjudicated segmentation before producing a measured denominator. Count distinct supported included members, not parser output rows or matching rules. Persist the exact support set in `CoverageReportSupport`; never recompute an old report against a newer inventory revision.

The [additional Bajaj and ICICI cases](../reference/additional-original-derived-cases-r8.jsonl) exercise annual versus disease-specific lifetime restoration, nonfungible component limits, and unresolved same-wording configuration selection. They supplement the fixed 400 and do not increase the 200-case acceptance denominator.

## Supplementary combi case: component request with package consequences

For `additional-r13-combi-01`, resolve the current owned bundle revision with its public wrapper configuration. Join its member slots to `TermsComponent`, the separately issued component revisions and their insured-member records, checking owner and product/issuer/version lineage. Fetch the wrapper identity, free-look scope/receipt rule, coupled-cancellation consequence and all refund/non-revival dependencies before explaining a life-only request. Return unresolved membership explicitly; never broaden the join to all policies sharing an insurer or name.

Bind the corrected receipt and transaction-kind assertions from the turn's customer profile revision, preserving the earlier upload as a separate event. Save dependencies on that profile revision, exact bundle/member revision artifacts and original rule/span hashes. A later receipt correction, membership revision or source conflict makes the prior answer stale. The old separately owned health policy is outside the selected package join. Reopening replays durable events and uses the corrected current profile revision; it does not repeat inference or reset receipt dates.
