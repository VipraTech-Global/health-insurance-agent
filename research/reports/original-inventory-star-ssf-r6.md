## Material interpretation findings

Silver uses a **per-hospitalization deductible**. Gold uses aggregate **admissible hospitalization expenses during the policy period** against a defined limit, with family SI floating across insured members and annual reset rules. Both exclude pre/posthospital expenses from threshold accumulation. Those exclusions do not mean all related pre/post expenses are themselves uninsured.

Modern cap ordering remains unresolved: the tables do not explicitly settle how procedure caps interact with threshold accumulation, final indemnity and other deductions. A table value alone is not a payment formula. The oral chemotherapy footnote expressly says **with or without hospitalization**, with pre/post included when hospitalization occurs; this exception must not be lost or extended to unstarred procedures.

Gold recharge expressly triggers after SI exhaustion/exceedance, once for the remaining period, can fund the same hospitalization or illness, and excludes modern treatments. General condition V.23 says SI exhaustion causes automatic expiry. Both must be loaded; the general condition cannot silently erase the specific benefit.

Further connected issues include registration/admission charges excluded by IV.33 but subsumed in treatment costs on page 10; newborn treatment excluded by IV.38 despite a midterm newborn wellness note; minimum 24-hour general admission versus **exceeding** 24 hours for modern inpatient treatment; Gold 15-day hospitalization notice alongside a general 24-hour event notice; biological inpatient restrictions versus modern daycare wording; delivery threshold/SI/lifetime-count interactions; and missing general entry-age/child-dependency parameters.

Some apparent tensions have explicit scope rules. Installment grace coverage operates notwithstanding contrary policy terms; ordinary renewal grace remains separate. Gold certified-copy evidence has a stated exception to general original-document requirements. Annual reset clauses prevent pooling SI or thresholds across multiple policy years. Each is recorded with its predicates, rather than treated as an unexplained contradiction.
